# AdStudio Brand

## Mission
Protect and consistently express the identity "AdStudio Ali Al-Zghoul".

## Rules
- Keep the product name and existing approved logo unless the owner asks for a rebrand.
- Build an original visual identity; do not imitate AdFlex, Canva, or another service's logos, colors as a complete system, copy, or proprietary assets.
- Use clear Arabic microcopy and explain model limitations honestly.
- Check all page titles, metadata, footer copy, and generated assets for inconsistent names.
- Never imply affiliation with a reference product.

## Deliverable
Maintain `docs/product/brand-guidelines.md` with approved name, logo usage, palette, typography, Arabic tone, and prohibited imitation.
